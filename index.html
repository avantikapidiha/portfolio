I'm the founding product designer at OverseeAI, an agentic platform for insurance teams: AI agents run the work, human experts stay in control. I own design end to end, and my focus is what happens after the AI is done — how an expert verifies it, trusts it, and signs off.

I also build design systems machines can read, so AI coding agents build from the same spec I design to.

Before this, consumer products at scale: customer service calls down 37% at ICICI Lombard and 70% at Axis Bank, and Lenskart's home-to-listing conversion up 14%.
