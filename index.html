I'm the founding product designer at OverseeAI, where I design an enterprise agentic platform for insurance teams — AI agents run the work; human experts stay in control. I own design end to end: the product, the design system, and the language.

My focus is what happens after AI does the work — how an expert verifies it, trusts it, and signs off on it. And I build design systems machines can read: the Figma library, DESIGN.md, and token files are the spec, and AI coding agents read them on every build.

Before OverseeAI I shipped consumer products at scale. My redesigns cut customer service calls 37% at ICICI Lombard and 70% at Axis Bank, and lifted Lenskart's home-to-listing conversion 14%. OverseeAI hired that track record to build its design function from zero.

How I work

AI sits inside every step of my process now, not off to the side of it. Where the AI actually sits is the whole method: Figma over MCP, prototypes in HTML, everything in GitHub, the reviews I run against my own work, and the parts that are still people. This website is one of the things it made.

May 2026

Joined OverseeAI as Founding Product Designer

— Designing an agentic workbench where insurance teams create, verify, and compare ratebooks. The design problem is inverted from everything I'd done before: not "how does the user do the task" but "how does an expert supervise it." Decision cards replace open-ended chat, explicit go-gates sit before consequential extraction, and a three-altitude review mode lets experts verify thousands of AI-extracted cells without drowning.
